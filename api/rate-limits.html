Rate limits

Rate limits protect database, queue and provider capacity so a traffic spike cannot compromise reconciliation correctness. Limits are deployment configuration and may differ by environment, tenant, credential and operation class.

What is limited

DimensionWhy it mattersClient behavior
Credential or tenantPrevents one integration from consuming shared capacity.Control concurrency per credential and tenant.
Route or operationProtects expensive queries and mutation paths.Use smaller pages and avoid polling loops.
AuthenticationSlows credential guessing and failure storms.Stop repeated attempts and investigate the source.
Provider or downstreamPrevents retries from amplifying an outage.Use bounded backoff and circuit breaking.

Handling 429

HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests
Retry-After: 10
Content-Type: application/json

{"error":{"code":"RATE_LIMITED","message":"Try again later.","request_id":"request_01J..."}}
  1. Stop sending requests to the limited route.
  2. Honor Retry-After when present.
  3. Otherwise use exponential backoff with jitter.
  4. Reduce concurrency and page size before resuming.
  5. Alert on sustained throttling instead of increasing retry volume.

Capacity-aware clients

Use a shared rate limiter for workers, not one limiter per thread. Keep read polling separate from write traffic, cache stable configuration, and schedule bulk reconciliation outside peak periods. Ask the deployment operator for the effective numeric limits rather than hard-coding values from another environment.
