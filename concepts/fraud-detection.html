Concepts

Fraud detection

Fraud detection separates an observable anomaly, a reviewable suspicion and a confirmed fraud decision. PesaGuard surfaces the first two with reasons and evidence; an authorized human owns the final decision and its consequences.

  1. A signal raises: named pattern, severity, transaction context.
  2. The right owner is reached through configured channels, with delivery attempts observable.
  3. A reviewer investigates and decides; the decision and reason land in the append-only trail.

Decision stages

StageMeaningControl
AnomalyA pattern differs from expected behavior.Show signal, evidence window and severity.
SuspicionA reviewer believes the activity needs investigation.Assign owner, preserve evidence and track age.
Confirmed fraudAn authorized decision records the conclusion.Require reason, actor, timestamp and follow-up action.

Evidence-led investigation

  • Compare the transaction with account, provider and channel history.
  • Check duplicate, velocity, timing and amount signals together.
  • Preserve the original payload and related audit events.
  • Separate a provider failure, reconciliation mismatch and fraud suspicion.
  • Record a decision that another reviewer can understand later.

Alert delivery and response

Alerts should identify the tenant, transaction, signal, severity and review link without including secrets or unnecessary personal data. Delivery retries must be observable and idempotent. A missing notification must not erase the anomaly from the review queue.
