Fraud detection Live

Fraud detection distinguishes anomaly from suspicion from confirmed fraud. PesaGuard's job is the first two — surfaced quickly, with reasons — while the third stays a recorded human decision.

  1. A signal raises: named pattern, severity, transaction context.
  2. The right owner is reached through configured channels, with delivery attempts observable.
  3. A reviewer investigates and decides; the decision and reason land in the append-only trail.
