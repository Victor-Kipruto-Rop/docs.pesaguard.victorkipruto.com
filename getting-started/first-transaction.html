Getting started

First transaction

A payment becomes a PesaGuard record in three steps: the callback is received and validated, the match is decided against your internal records, and the outcome keeps its evidence.

Follow the evidence

Your first transaction is successful when the complete path is visible: callback, durable record, match decision, audit event and downstream notification.

INGESTMATCHEXPLAIN

1 · The callback arrives

Safaricom Daraja delivers a callback to your deployment. Ingestion validates the payload, normalizes it into the canonical payment-event shape, and de-duplicates it on write.

  • Invalid payloads are quarantined with the reason — never dropped silently.
  • A provider retry of the same event is rejected by idempotency, not processed twice.

2 · The match is decided

The event is compared against internal records on amount, reference and a timestamp tolerance. Deterministic rules mean the same inputs always produce the same outcome.

  • Matched — the record carries the evidence that produced the decision.
  • Difference found — an exception is raised with the delta shown.
  • No candidate — the payment queues as unmatched with the reason attached.

3 · The outcome is recorded

Every outcome is written to the append-only audit trail with its actor and tenant. A reviewer resolving an exception records the decision and the reason, which keeps month-end explainable.

First-transaction verification

EvidenceWhat to confirm
Provider eventRaw callback is authenticated, validated and assigned an event identity.
Durable recordDuplicate delivery does not create a second financial record.
Match resultAmount, reference and time evidence explain the outcome.
Audit eventTenant, actor, reason and correlation ids are present.
NotificationDownstream delivery is observable and retry-safe.

If the first transaction does not match

Do not edit the record to force success. Inspect the exception reason, confirm the internal reference and amount, then follow the reviewer workflow in Reconciliation.
