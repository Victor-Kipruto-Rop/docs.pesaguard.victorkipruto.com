Reconciliation guides

Exceptions

An exception means a candidate exists but the evidence does not satisfy the matching contract. The difference remains explicit and a reviewer records the decision, reason and next action.

Exception review workflow

  1. Claim or assign the exception without hiding it from other operators.
  2. Review provider payload, internal record and matching evidence.
  3. Classify amount, reference, timing, duplicate or configuration difference.
  4. Accept, reject, correct or escalate with a written reason.
  5. Verify the state, report and audit trail after resolution.

Review evidence

EvidenceWhy it matters
Amount and currencyShows the financial delta without silent rounding.
Provider referenceConnects the external event to the internal candidate.
Timestamp windowExplains late delivery and clock differences.
Actor and reasonMakes the resolution auditable.

Escalate when

Escalate repeated exceptions, unexplained financial deltas, cross-tenant anomalies, suspected credential misuse or a rule that would change historical outcomes. Do not resolve uncertainty by editing the database directly.

Differences are first-class: the delta is stated and a reviewer decides with a written reason.
