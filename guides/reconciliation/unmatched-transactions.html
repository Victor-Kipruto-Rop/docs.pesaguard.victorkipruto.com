Reconciliation guides

Unmatched transactions

An unmatched transaction has no valid internal candidate at the time of evaluation. It remains visible with a reason, tenant context and provider evidence so late data or configuration issues can be investigated.

Unmatched is a state, not a loss

Keep the event, expose the reason and make reprocessing safe when the missing evidence arrives.

QUEUEEXPLAINREPLAY

Investigation steps

  1. Open the provider event and confirm its tenant and identity.
  2. Read the unmatched reason and candidate-search window.
  3. Check internal reference, amount, currency and timing.
  4. Confirm whether the internal record arrived late or belongs to another flow.
  5. Reprocess with the original event identity after correction.

Do not discard

Do not delete an unmatched event, alter its original payload or create a manual success without an audit decision. Unmatched volume and age are operational signals that should appear in queues and reports.

Queue with the reason attached: never silently discarded.
