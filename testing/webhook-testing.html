Testing

Webhook testing

Test the receiver as an independent reliability boundary: verify signatures before parsing, acknowledge only durable acceptance, handle duplicates and prove replay from failure states.

Receiver readiness

A webhook consumer is ready when it is secure, idempotent, observable and safe to replay.

Webhook test matrix

ScenarioExpected behavior
Valid signatureRaw body verifies, event is durably accepted and acknowledgement follows persistence.
Invalid signatureReject before parsing or business processing.
Duplicate deliveryOne durable event and one downstream effect.
Consumer outageProvider retry or dead letter preserves delivery identity.
Malformed payloadSafe rejection with evidence and no partial financial effect.
Out-of-order eventState guards prevent a transaction from regressing.

Test procedure

  1. Deploy the staging consumer with a staging signing secret.
  2. Capture the raw body before JSON parsing.
  3. Send a valid event and assert signature, persistence and acknowledgement.
  4. Repeat the delivery and confirm idempotent handling.
  5. Force a consumer failure and observe retry or dead-letter behavior.
  6. Replay the event and compare final state, audit and notification evidence.

Observability checklist

  • Delivery id, event id and tenant are logged safely.
  • Signature failures are counted without logging secrets.
  • Retry count, age and dead-letter volume are measurable.
  • Processing latency and acknowledgement status are visible.
  • Replayed events remain linked to their original identity.
