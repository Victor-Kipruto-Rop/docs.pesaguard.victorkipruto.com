Webhooks

Event catalog Draft

Events come from the reconciliation pipeline and the operations surface. The catalog below names the families; exact payload schemas come from the OpenAPI document on your deployment.

FamilyProduced when
Delivery eventsA Daraja callback is received, processed, retried or dead-lettered.
Reconciliation outcomesA match is decided, or an exception is raised and resolved.
Anomaly signalsA rule or statistical check flags a transaction for review.
Notification eventsAn alert is routed to SMS, email, Slack or a webhook consumer.
