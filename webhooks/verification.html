Webhooks

Verification

Every outbound delivery is signed. Your consumer should reject any delivery whose signature does not verify: the signature is what turns an HTTP POST into a trustworthy event.

  1. Read the signature header from the delivery.
  2. Recompute the signature over the raw body using your shared secret.
  3. Compare with a constant-time comparison; reject on mismatch with 401.
  4. Only then parse the body and act: and act idempotently.
Trust boundary

Signature verification must happen before parsing, persistence or business logic.

Read raw bytesDo not reserialize JSON before calculating the digest.
Resolve secretUse the environment-specific signing secret from protected storage.
CalculateApply the documented algorithm and canonicalization exactly.
CompareUse constant-time comparison and reject malformed headers.
ProcessParse and act only after verification; retain delivery identity.

Failure response

Reject an invalid signature before parsing or invoking business logic. Record a safe delivery id and verification outcome, but never log the shared secret, full signature or sensitive payload.
